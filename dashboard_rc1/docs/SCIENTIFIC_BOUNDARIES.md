# Scientific boundaries

- Never place PISA and PIRLS raw score points on a common achievement scale.
- PISA Level 2 and PIRLS 475 are instrument-specific thresholds.
- Cross-age pairs are repeated cross-sections, not the same children.
- School sorting is descriptive; it is not itself a causal tracking estimate.
- National-relative SES and international ESCS are distinct analyses.
- Language/migration diagnostics remain conditional and can be non-identical concepts across surveys.
- Representativeness warnings travel with the relevant result.
- Ranks are descriptive point-estimate ranks; no rank-confidence inference is displayed.
